# Tiny Explorer — Episode Bible

Every episode teaches **one thing to learn** (numbers, colours, shapes, words) and **one lesson for life** (sharing, saying sorry, trying again), wrapped in a tiny story with Bobo and his friend Pip. Kids talk back; parents feel good pressing play.

Built with `generator/episode_engine.py`: write an episode spec (a short Python list of beats), the engine renders the 16:9 episode, two vertical Shorts and the thumbnail.

## What the research says, and what we do about it

| Principle | Where it comes from | Our rule |
|---|---|---|
| Big, expressive faces hold toddler attention | Child-development experts on CoComelon | Bobo and Pip are large on screen, with clear moods: happy, surprised, sad, excited |
| A ~3-second pause after a prompt is where language happens | Ms Rachel's technique, speech-language practice | Every "Ask" beat waits 3 full seconds with a visible countdown before the answer |
| Repetition and predictable structure help learning | Same sources | Same 8-beat structure every episode, same chorus melody, same catchphrases |
| Gentle constant motion, not overload | Experts on pacing | Slow camera drift, bouncing on the beat, no flashing, no fast cuts |
| Kids learn emotional tools from small stories | Why Bluey works for parents | A small problem, a feeling named, a simple tool, a happy fix |
| Learning sticks when a grown-up joins in | Joint-attention research | At least one "Do it with your grown-up!" prompt per episode |

## The cast

- **Bobo** (hero): round orange explorer, tan safari hat, magnifying glass. Curious, gentle, a bit silly, always encouraging. Speaks in warm low "Bobo-speak" blips.
- **Pip** (friend): small sky-blue bird with a yellow beak and a little tuft. Excitable, sometimes worried or sad; the one who has the problem. Higher, quicker blips.
- **The viewer** is the third friend. Bobo talks straight to them.

## Episode structure (about 2.5 minutes)

1. **Intro** (3.5 s): branded Tiny Explorer sting.
2. **Hello** (~8 s): Bobo waves to camera: "Hi, explorers! It's me, Bobo!"
3. **Problem** (~15 s): Pip has a small problem and a feeling (sad, scared, cross, stuck). Name the feeling.
4. **Learn** (~45 s): the learning topic as a counting/naming song. Items pop in on the beat with the chorus "One, two, three, explore with me!"
5. **Ask** (~35 s): three questions to the viewer, each with a 3-second countdown and a cheer.
6. **Lesson** (~25 s): Bobo uses a simple tool to fix the problem (shares, says sorry, tries again). Pip is happy. Lesson card in big words.
7. **Do it with your grown-up** (~10 s): one action at home ("Share a toy with someone today!").
8. **Recap + Bye** (~15 s): chorus once more, then "Bye-bye, explorers!" with a wave.

Shorts: 25–35 s vertical, one Ask moment from the episode, hook in the first 2 seconds ("Can you count Bobo's apples?").

## Lessons rotation (pair each with a learning topic)

| Lesson | Problem Pip has | Tool Bobo shows |
|---|---|---|
| Sharing | Pip has no apples | Share some, both are happy |
| Saying sorry | Bobo knocks over Pip's tower | Say sorry and rebuild together |
| Trying again | Pip can't fly to the high branch | Try, try again, one more time |
| Taking turns | Both want the swing | You go, then me |
| Being brave | Pip is scared of the dark | Deep breath, a little light, a friend |
| Please and thank you | Pip grabs the ball | Ask nicely, say thank you |
| Helping | Leaves everywhere after the wind | Help tidy, it's faster together |
| Naming feelings | Pip is cross but won't say why | "I feel cross because…" |
| Washing hands | Muddy hands before snack | Wash, count to 20, dry |
| Patience | Waiting for the cake to bake | Count, sing, wait |
| Kindness to animals | A ladybug on the path | Gentle hands, let it go |
| Bedtime routine | Pip won't sleep | Bath, book, cuddle, lights out |

## Catchphrases (use every episode)

- "Hi, explorers!" (open)
- "Let's explore!" (start of Learn)
- "Your turn!" (before each countdown)
- "Yay, you did it!" (after each answer)
- "Bye-bye, explorers!" (close)

## Sound

- Signature chorus melody in C major, 120 BPM, played on bells in every Learn and Recap beat.
- Music ducks under dialogue; full volume in songs.
- Sound effects: pop for each item, tick for countdowns, sparkle for reveals, gentle cheer for answers, soft descending notes for a sad moment (never scary).
- No copyrighted music, no real voices yet; words are on screen in speech bubbles with Bobo-speak blips.

## Visual rules

- Scenes: meadow, orchard, beach, cosy room, starry night. One scene per beat group, gentle white dip between scenes.
- Bobo bottom-left during learning and asking (YouTube watermark sits bottom-right).
- Text: Poppins Bold, dark purple outline, big enough to read on a phone across a room.
- Never: scary imagery, fast flashing, real children, brand characters, claims like "makes your child smarter".
