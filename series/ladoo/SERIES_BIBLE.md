# Ladoo Comes Home — Series Bible

A Tiny Explorer story series about **Ladoo**, a golden Indian indie puppy adopted from a shelter, and the family who love him. Every episode shows the love between a rescued dog and his people, with one small, true-to-life moment of puppy life.

- **Channel:** Tiny Explorer (YouTube), ages 1–5, watched with parents
- **Schedule:** two new episodes a week, **Wednesday and Saturday at 5:00 PM IST**, each with one vertical Short (published the same day at 12:00 PM)
- **Length:** 2–3 minutes, 16:9; Short 20–35 s, 9:16
- **Engine:** `generator/ladoo_engine.py` (reuses the Tiny Explorer world, intro sting, fonts and bubbles); scripts live in `series/ladoo/lNNN_<slug>.py`
- **Heart of the show:** dogs love their families completely, and families who adopt give a puppy a home. Gentle "adopt, don't shop" spirit for parents, never preachy.

## Every episode

1. Tiny Explorer intro sting (automatic) → title card "Ladoo Comes Home" + episode name
2. A small everyday situation for a new puppy (first night, bath, a walk, a loud noise, a visitor)
3. A feeling named (Ladoo is scared, lonely, excited, confused) using his thought cloud
4. **At least 2 "ask" beats** to the viewer (How is Ladoo feeling? What should Kabir do?) with the 3-second countdown
5. The family responds with love and the right, safe way to care for a dog
6. A **moment** beat: the love shown (licks, cuddles, tail wags, hearts)
7. Lesson card in big words
8. **"With your grown-up!"** beat with a dog-safety or kindness tip
9. Bye card with "Next time: <next episode>"

## Safety rules for dog content (always)

- Always ask a grown-up before touching any dog; let a dog sniff your hand first; gentle hands, no pulling ears or tails
- Never disturb a dog that is eating or sleeping
- Ladoo is never hurt, punished, shouted at, abandoned or in danger for more than a few seconds; scary moments resolve fast and kindly
- Show good care: water, vet visits, walks on a lead, cleaning up, a cosy bed. No feeding chocolate or sweets to Ladoo (his name is a sweet, but he never eats one)
- Never: real dogs' or children's photos, brand characters, claims about breeds being better or worse, fear about stray dogs

## Writing notes

- Short lines (under 60 characters a bubble), toddler words, warm tone
- Ladoo's thought clouds carry the emotion: "I hope someone picks me...", "Is this my home now?"
- Kabir makes the toddler-sized mistakes and learns; Anya explains; parents never shout
- Use the catchphrases: "Hi, explorers!" (narrator or Anya), "Your turn!", "Yay, you did it!", "Bye-bye, explorers!"
