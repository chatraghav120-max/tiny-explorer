# Mishti to the Rescue! — Series Bible

A Tiny Explorer story series about **Tara** (5) and her best friend **Mishti**, a brave little hero cat. Mishti's gold bell glows whenever danger is near, and she always keeps Tara safe. The "evil" is never scary: real everyday dangers (a busy road, a hot pot, an open door) and **Grumbo**, a grumpy raincloud who makes little troubles and, over the season, becomes a friend.

- **Channel:** Tiny Explorer (YouTube), ages 1–5, watched with parents
- **Schedule:** two episodes a week, **Tuesday and Friday at 5:00 PM IST**, each with one vertical Short (12:00 PM the same day). Ladoo Comes Home has Wednesday and Saturday.
- **Length:** 2–3 minutes, 16:9; Short 20–35 s, 9:16
- **Engine:** `generator/ladoo_engine.py` (the shared story engine), with `HERO = "mishti"` in each script. Scripts: `series/mishti/mNNN_<slug>.py`
- **Heart of the show:** the bond between a child and her cat, and simple safety habits kids can copy. Parents get a show that teaches "stop, ask a grown-up" without fear.

## The cast

| Character | Who | Look | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mishti** | Hero cat | Grey tabby, white chest and muzzle, pink ears, little red cape, red collar with a gold bell | Brave, calm, loving; purrs and meows (only "Meow!" out loud), thinks in purple thought clouds. Her bell glows (act "glow") when danger is near |
| **Tara** | 5-year-old girl | Curly black hair, purple dress | Curious and quick, sometimes rushes; learns to stop and ask |

| **Grumbo** | Grumpy raincloud | Grey cloud, cross eyebrows, light rain; turns white and smiling when happy | Grumbles "Hmph!", blows toys away or makes things dark because he's lonely. Never scary, never hurts anyone. Befriended in Ep 12 |

Character art is code only (`mishti()`, `grumbo()`, `person()` in the engine). Never draw them another way.

## Every episode

1. Tiny Explorer intro sting → title card "Mishti to the Rescue!" + episode name
2. Tara and Mishti playing happily
3. Trouble starts (a real danger, often nudged by Grumbo)
4. **Ask** the viewer: "Should Tara…?" (answer: "No! Stop!")
5. **Rescue** beat: Mishti's bell glows, she leaps, a big burst word ("STOP!", "WAIT!", "HOT!")
6. Mishti's thought cloud says the safety rule in kid words; a grown-up confirms and praises
7. Grumbo grumbles; Tara invites him to be kind (his arc softens each episode)
8. **Moment**: Tara hugs Mishti, hearts ("My hero, Mishti!")
9. Lesson card → "With your grown-up!" safety tip → bye with "Next time: …"
At least 2 ask beats per episode.

## Safety and tone rules (always)

- Dangers are shown before anything bad happens; nobody gets hurt, burned, lost for long or frightened for more than a few seconds
- The safe action is always: stop, ask a grown-up, hold hands, wait. Tara is praised, never scolded
- No strangers as villains, no kidnapping, no weapons, no monsters, no darkness that lingers. Grumbo is silly, not evil
- Real-world accuracy: hold hands to cross, look left and right, never touch hot pots, never eat unknown berries or medicine, swim only with a grown-up, ask before opening the door
- Be gentle with cats: no pulling tails, let them come to you
- Never: real children's or pets' photos, brand characters, fear-based messages to parents

## Writing notes

- Bubble lines under ~60 characters; toddler words
- Rescue burst words: STOP!, WAIT!, HOT!, NO NO!, CAREFUL!
- Catchphrases: "Mishti to the rescue!", Tara's "My hero, Mishti!", Grumbo's "Hmph!", plus the channel's "Your turn!", "Yay, you did it!", "Bye-bye, explorers!"
